How the score works

Exact rules and input guide

A 2–10 is worth 10 base points, J–K 30, and A 50. Treasure suit adds +1×. Trash suit subtracts 1×, except an all-trash hand receives +1× instead. Exactly two of a rank each gain +0.5×; three or more each gain +1×. Jokers choose the single best virtual card, and only one Joker scores. Cash adds one point per dollar.

Card entry needs your real hand. Cash and Joker counts must reflect your current holdings. Unknown contracts count only in estimated score, never in confirmed score.

Auction bid calculator

Three highest bids, including tied bidders, buy the item and must pay. The five auctions start at 0:00, 3:00, 6:00, 9:00, and 12:00. Each closes 2:40 later.

0% uses expected contract payout. 100% subtracts one estimated standard deviation of the contract's payout. This does not predict winning bids.

If you win, pay the bid at the booth no later than the end of the next auction, or risk disqualification. For auction 5, pay after trading ends if needed.

Joker economics

Bid strategy

Value comes from your actual hand. If you already own a Joker, another Joker adds no scoring value while you keep the first. For a contract, use its printed payout and your estimated probability of satisfying its condition. Do not bid more cash than you own.

Winning cutoff observations give a rough indication of rival demand, not a reliable forecast. Teams can change behavior during the round.
