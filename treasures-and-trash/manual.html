AT A GLANCE

One team, three desks

The trading round has four panel stages: 1. Hand & prices → 2. Trade calculator → 3. Auctions → 4. Opponents. Cards, cash and contracts are shared team resources. The panel evaluates marginal score changes but cannot negotiate or deliver assets on its own.

Privacy rule: the competition prohibits showing your private cards to other teams. Keep the Hand screen visible only to your officially assigned teammates. Other teams may ask to see your cash, Jokers and contracts, which are public assets.

Each of three rounds has a two-minute discussion period followed by a 15-minute trading period. Your team starts each round with nine cards, $500 and 15 auction slips. The competition has 40 teams.

BEFORE THE FIRST TRADE

Prepare your hand, cash and suit

  1. Open the local panel. Run python -m http.server 8000 in the repository root and open http://localhost:8000/treasures-and-trash/. Keep it on a team-only device, away from rival view. Use the official organizer's announcements for timing.
  2. Select the correct round and treasure suit. In the header choose Round 1 and the announced Treasure suit (Hearts, Diamonds, Spades or Clubs). The suit of the same color is trash; the two opposite-color suits are neutral.
  3. Record your initial hand. Open 1. Hand & prices. Use + Add cards to tap each suit and rank, or expand Faster: paste your full hand at once. Use Replace hand after verifying all nine cards, or Add these cards to augment the hand. Example format: AH 10S 3D 4C 4H 2xKC.
  4. Confirm cash and special assets. Check Cash, $ starts at 500 and Owned Jokers matches reality, normally zero. If you already hold any bounty contracts, use Current contracts → Add a contract you already own, supply its name, payout and estimated completion chance.
  5. Decide who physically holds what. One teammate keeps the card hand and confirms exchanges, another negotiates offers, and another tracks cash, auction slips and the on-screen calculator. If only two team members are present, the negotiator also handles auctions while the calculator operator remains with the hand.
TEAM OF THREE

Keep transactions and bookkeeping separate

PLAYER A · HAND / CALCULATOR

Protect and value holdings

Own the panel and physical card list. Check Top cards to buy, Cards you can sell, and the Trade calculator. No trade is committed until Player A checks the actual cards received/given and score difference.

PLAYER B · NEGOTIATOR

Find and negotiate offers

Speak with other teams. Ask what card or asset they will exchange and at what price. Relay exact proposals without displaying your private hand. Get Player A's approval before promising a trade. After acceptance, deliver/receive exactly the agreed assets.

PLAYER C · AUCTIONS / CASH

Watch the booths and budget

Track auction numbers and closes, carry auction slips, estimate Joker/bounty bid value, and monitor cash reserved for won but unpaid lots. Pay obligations before deadlines and report actual cash spent and assets obtained.

TWO-PLAYER FALLBACK

One ledger, one field operator

Player A maintains the panel, current hand, cash and approvals. Player B negotiates and takes auction slips to booths. Before a commitment, read back all terms to Player A.

Simple rule: the teammate controlling the ledger must confirm every physical exchange and auction payment. Otherwise the engine may recommend selling a card that is no longer held or spending cash already owed.

EACH ROUND

Exact panel workflow

  1. Stage 1: value the current hand. Open 1. Hand & prices, check all suit/rank entries and cash. Price the next card displays the exact marginal point gain of adding a card to your current hand, not the price a rival will accept. Top cards to buy shows modeled break-even purchase prices, and Cards you can sell shows minimum break-even prices. Click Try on a card to load it into the trade editor.
  2. Stage 2: evaluate a full proposal. In 2. Trade calculator, use TAP CARDS INSTEAD OF TYPING CODES: select RECEIVE from rival or GIVE to rival, tap a suit and rank to assemble the card bundle, and tap any selected card marked × to remove it. Cash shortcuts such as Pay $25 or Get $25 populate the cash field. The editable text fields below remain available for bulk codes. Use signed Cash change ($), where positive is cash you receive and negative is cash you pay. Set Jokers change positive when receiving and negative when giving. Under Include contracts in this trade, specify any bounty given or received and its payout/chance. Read the projected exact score change, including same-rank bonuses and all-trash effects.
  3. Execute only a real agreement. Player B reads the complete proposal aloud. Player A checks hand inventory, remaining cash and calculated delta. Then Player B physically completes the negotiated exchange. Only after both sides exchanged the agreed assets, click Record this trade after agreement. Use Clear proposal if the deal was rejected.
  4. Stage 3: price auctions. Open 3. Auctions, select Auction number 1–5 and Joker or Bounty contract. For a bounty, enter the printed title/condition, payout and your estimated success probability. Check Downside sensitivity, the proposed entry bid, and the maximum economically defensible bid. Enter the real sealed bid on a slip at the booth, not in the panel.
  5. After auction outcome, separate observation from ownership. If you learn an actual historical winning cutoff, enter Observed 3rd-place winning bid ($) and click Save observed cutoff. This is only evidence. If you actually won, enter Actual winning bid you paid ($), settle the payment with the booth, then click Record won item and cash paid. This changes cash and owned items. Do not use it for a mere observation.
  6. Stage 4: learn from rival offers. Open 4. Opponents. Enter Other team / identifier, choose whether they want to BUY this asset from you or SELL this asset to you, then enter card/asset and offered price. Record outcome Offer only, Accepted or Rejected, and click Record observation. The Opponent counterplay section can reprioritize currently open offers; confirm the rival still honors the offer before acting.
  7. Return to Stage 1 after every trade. Confirm physical cards, remaining cash, Jokers and contracts match the panel. Re-evaluate the best card targets and send Player B a new short list. If the physical trade did not happen, never record it as completed.
  8. End the trading phase. When the official 15-minute trading window closes, stop proposing trades. Reconcile assets and submit them to the executive for scoring. Choose the next round and new treasure suit only after the organizer announces them.

COMMON MISTAKES

Corrections without duplicate assets

ProblemCorrect response
Physical hand and panel differUse + Add cards, − Remove cards, or a verified Replace hand. Recount all cards before another trade.
Trade proposal changed or was refusedUse Clear proposal. Do not click Record this trade after agreement.
Accidentally recorded a transactionUse Undo last recorded action when available and check resulting holdings, cash, Jokers and contracts.
Rival changes an offerRecord the new price or outcome in 4. Opponents. Historical observations are not guaranteed current orders.
Winning bid exceeds available cashThe auction's official obligation still applies. Immediately reconcile outstanding payment and contact the organizer if necessary; the model cannot void a bid.
Need a clean restartClick Reset entire game in the toolbar and confirm; it clears all three local Treasures & Trash rounds, not the real exchange.
STOP TRADING

Final physical inventory check

  • All starting and traded cards are accounted for; no private cards were shown to other teams.
  • Cash equals initial cash plus received cash minus paid trades and auction payments.
  • All won Jokers and bounty contracts are present and owed auction payments are settled.
  • Any trade you recorded was physically completed, never merely discussed or accepted in principle.
  • Score is reviewed for treasure/trash multipliers, matching ranks and the all-trash exception.
  • Team submits its assets when instructed and switches to the next round's announced treasure suit.
