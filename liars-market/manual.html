AT A GLANCE

One continuous sub-game at a time

For each dealt hand, complete the panel in order: 1. Deal cards → 2. Claims & calls → 3. Record result. The panel automatically advances to the next sub-game when you record a result. Use Update cards / reveal center if another face-down center card is turned up.

This is a two-person physical card game, with 3 rounds at tables of four teams. Each round has timed turns, 4 extension cards per team and a local scoreboard. Your official proctor decides which claim, challenge and result is valid.

BEFORE PLAY

Create a reliable table map

  1. Open the panel. Run python -m http.server 8000 from the repository root, then open http://localhost:8000/liars-market/. Keep the screen visible to your teammate but out of sight of opposing teams.
  2. Pick the round and correct hand. At the top choose Round 1–3 and the current Sub-game. Select Hands / round to match the proctor's instructions. The packet says eight hands in its overview but five in the detailed procedure, so confirm which is used at your table; the panel supports both.
  3. Fix the seat mapping. In 1. Deal cards → Scores before this hand, select My seat (Seat 1, 2, 3 or 4) and record each team's current points, not points from another round. Keep the physical clockwise seat order consistent for the entire round.
  4. Enter every private card you hold. Under Your cards and visible center, choose My hand and tap cards in the picker, including a Joker if present. Choose Face-up center and tap the three visible common cards. Do not enter the two center cards face down until they are turned up. Use Clear hand or Clear center to correct a mistake.
  5. Check entry completeness. Read the stage's card-count warning and the expected hand size from current scores. Do not proceed on a wrong private hand count. Click Cards entered. Start claims → when the cards and seats match the physical table.
Your hidden cards and another team's hidden cards are different information. Only enter cards visible to your own team, or publicly revealed to the entire table. The panel cannot see unseen opponent cards; its probabilities are conditional estimates.
TEAM OF TWO

Separate card entry from spoken decisions

YOU · CARD / MODEL OPERATOR

Keep the panel current

Update score and seat order, enter your private and visible center cards, transcribe each spoken claim, identify the claimant, and read back estimated BLUFF/NUTS/PASS values and legal next claims. Handle the local result journal.

TEAMMATE · TABLE SPEAKER / CLOCK

Act at the actual table

Listen to the proctor and the other three teams. Tell the panel operator each claim exactly. Make the actual BLUFF/NUTS/PASS choice or hand claim within the time limit. Announce center reveals and the official result after the hand ends.

Fast communication: the speaking player immediately announces claimant seat and claim, then the panel operator reads back the estimated call and its confidence. The speaking player independently checks that it is your team's reaction turn and that the actual time remains. The algorithm does not replace table judgment.

For one-person teams, use the same sequence but prioritize the official 10-second reaction and 15-second claim clocks. If there isn't time for panel entry, take a legal action at the table first and backfill the result later.

EVERY SUB-GAME

Exact controls and transitions

  1. Stage 1: reset the hand context. At the start of each new sub-game, check Scores before this hand, seat mapping, and the number of private cards dealt to each team. Score deficits can give teams more cards, so stale scores change the model. Use My hand to enter every card dealt to you and Face-up center to enter three initially revealed common cards.
  2. Stage 2: capture a spoken claim immediately. Open 2. Claims & calls. Under Latest spoken claim, the Tap to build a claim (no shorthand typing) panel is expanded by default. Tap the class (for example Two pair or Full house), tap the main rank and second rank if required, then choose Set as latest claim. Tap WHO SPOKE? and the correct Seat 1–4 to identify the claimant. The shorthand input remains an optional fallback.
  3. Read the actual reaction recommendation. The top decision card shows BLUFF, NUTS or PASS, with modeled probabilities and expected point changes under Probability and counterplay. Only make a challenge when it is your seat's reaction priority. “PASS” means decline the current reaction; it does not mean the current claim is automatically correct.
  4. If it is your claim turn, choose a legal raise. Scroll to Top legal claims and consider a suggested hand that ranks strictly above the previous claim. Tap Use as claim to make the selected recommendation the panel's latest claim. Your teammate must speak the full hand class and significant ranks at the table. Tapping the app does not speak or submit anything.
  5. If the proctor reveals another center card, update stage 1. Choose Update cards / reveal center, then Face-up center and select the newly turned-up card. Return to 2. Claims & calls. The panel recalculates the unknown-card scenarios. The two initially face-down cards are revealed before the marker team's second and third turns.
  6. Use the local timer correctly. Under Reaction timer, use Reaction 10s for a response, Claim 15s for a turn, or Extension 30s after an officially spent +15s extension. Press Stop if timing changes. Update the extension count via Use 1 on the relevant seat only after that team spends one. Each team has four per round.
  7. Stage 3: record the actual outcome. Once a challenge or forced ending settles the hand, click Someone called / hand ended → Record result. Under How did this hand end?, tap a BLUFF correct/wrong, NUTS correct/wrong or timeout button, then tap the OTHER TEAM seat. The dropdowns still work. If applicable answer If NUTS was wrong: did claim exist? according to what was actually revealed. Check the Point transfer preview.
  8. Advance only after verifying scores. Click Record result + next. The app saves the point transfer and opens the next hand's Deal cards stage automatically. To fix an incorrect last result, use Undo latest result. After the final hand in a round, the panel advances to the next round where applicable.

Example outcomes above are illustrative. The speaker must repeat any recommendation in human terms before acting. The operator should announce if the card set is incomplete or the table's action order differs from the panel's current seat/claim state.

Risk checks for BLUFF vs NUTS

  • BLUFF claims the named hand is absent from all active cards. It loses if the claim actually exists. Jokers are wild and may help produce it.
  • NUTS claims the hand both exists and is the strongest available among active cards. An existing but nonmaximal hand makes a Nuts challenge incorrect.
  • PASS does not reveal anything. If you cannot verify an unseen hand, the model estimates probability rather than certainty.
  • Legal raising order differs from poker: six of a kind, five of a kind, straight flush, four of a kind, full house, three of a kind, two pair, straight, pair, then high card. There is no ordinary flush hand class.
  • Timeouts have consequences: missing your claim turn loses the hand (except the first turn, which defaults to High Card 2); missing a reaction is treated as passing.

BEFORE THE NEXT DEAL

Completion checklist

  • Actual final spoken claim and claimant seat match the panel.
  • BLUFF/NUTS/forced ending is recorded exactly as the proctor adjudicated.
  • Point transfer preview matches the official scoring of the hand.
  • One Record result + next action was made, with no duplicate result.
  • Next hand's card counts were recomputed from updated scores, and extension counts remain accurate.
  • New center and private card entries are made only from what the next hand reveals.
