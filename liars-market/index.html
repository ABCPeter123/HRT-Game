How to use every input, scoring and limits

Round / sub-game: choose the current session. The overview says 8 sub-games per round; the detailed rules say 5. Ask the proctor which applies.

Latest claim: enter a type and the specified rank(s), using short notation or the claim builder. Full house uses trip rank first, pair rank second. Two pair uses high pair first. An SF or straight uses its top rank, and A-2-3-4-5 is 5-high.

Claimant: seat who said the current claim. This selects their learned claim credibility, if past outcomes exist.

My hand: your current private cards. Face-up center: all revealed shared center cards. Tap a card a second time to remove it. The same physical card cannot occur twice. Enter jokers explicitly.

Scoreboard: point totals before this sub-game. The deck count per team is 7 at ≤−6, 6 at ≤−4, 5 at ≤−2, 4 at −1..1, 3 at ≥2, 2 at ≥4, 1 at ≥6. The proctor initially deals 3 visible and 2 hidden center cards.

Downside sensitivity: penalizes calls and claims with more risk of losing points. It does not guarantee safety.

Outcome / Other team: select the confirmed challenge result and the team that won or lost against the claimant. This transfers the rule-defined points; reverse with Undo. Unknown failed-NUTS truth is not treated as verified evidence.

Probabilities: Monte Carlo worlds uniformly draw unknown active cards from the unseen deck. They do not know hidden opponents' cards or how opponents select claims. The assumption may be inaccurate. Each decision uses 400 reproducible local draws.

NUTS: the claim must exist and no hand above it may exist. Jokers are wild. Hand order is six, five, straight flush, four, full house, three, two pair, straight, pair, high card. Ordinary flushes do not count.

Scoring: successful BLUFF takes 3 for low hands, 2 for full house / four, 1 for straight flush or above. Failed BLUFF pays claimant 1, 2, 3 respectively. Correct NUTS takes 4; incorrect NUTS pays 1.

Finals: the packet changes to two-life elimination without points/catch-up. This panel models regular scored rounds only.

All inputs stay in this browser. No competition connection, order submission, or Vercel deployment is used.
