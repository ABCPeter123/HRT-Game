Rules, assumptions and how every input works

Maker / taker roles: select the maker cohort assigned by the proctor. The role follows rounds 1 and 4 or rounds 2 and 3. Four rounds, each 10:30.

Chips: 30 independent integer values from 1–10. Chips 1–20 are public, 21–30 are visible only to takers. A numeric partial is 50% true, 25% within ±2 of true, and 15% incorrect; known combination chips show the rounded mean of two other chips and have 10% incidence. The mixture is conditional on the observed numeric type. Unknown hidden chips use uniform priors.

Settlement: VAULT = sum of 30 true values; PUB = sum of public 20 true values; G5 = count of all 30 true values greater than 5. Event-bot trades NEVER change settlement in Vault.

Book: Best bid is the highest displayed buy price; best ask is the lowest sell price. Sizes are visible shares at these levels. Position is already-filled shares; working buys/sells are outstanding unfilled shares.

Orders: Makers may post passive limits and take liquidity. Takers may only use immediate-or-cancel. Price bands: VAULT $30–300, G5 $0–30, PUB $20–200. Tick sizes: $0.25 for VAULT and PUB, $0.10 for G5. Per-asset limits: ±500 makers and ±300 takers including worst-case outstanding fills.

Maker penalty: for each contract, $0.01 per second times excess of qualifying own quote width over Wmax. Each own side must have at least 10 outstanding shares, otherwise the exchange band endpoint substitutes. Wmax is a standard deviation calculated using only exact public chip values makers have received. Penalty does not apply during first 10 seconds and is waived for 5 seconds after a >10-share resting quote was filled or chipped. Timer-based penalty accrual is a local estimate.

Model: Values come from an approximate marginal posterior including combination constraints. Correlations from combination chips are not fully represented in uncertainty. Suggested orders account for observed executable top price, uncertainty, your risk setting, and inventory capacity. No specific customer fill rate is claimed.

Live capture: optional read-only local numeric bridge can populate book prices from user-mapped visible DOM elements. It does not reveal hidden chip data, connect to the exchange, or place orders. Prices must be checked against the real interface.

Optional. Run node vault/bridge/server.mjs, reload the Vault Chrome extension, and choose Follow contract dynamically for reorder-safe prices. The reader imports visible DOM quotes only. Manual entry always works.
All decisions are local and advisory. No order is submitted to the real game. Production Vercel deployments remain paused.
